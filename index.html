My path into AI wasn't exactly a straight line, and honestly that's the part I like most about it.

I started out in Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, then spent a couple of years at TCS automating enterprise workflows before moving to the US for my Master's in Data Science at the University of Oklahoma. A year of neuroimaging research as a Research Assistant got me hooked on working with messy, real-world data, and as generative AI took off I found myself pulled toward building actual AI products. These days I'm an AI Engineering Intern at The Aether Loop, working on multi-agent systems, model evaluation, and the cloud infrastructure behind it all.

I like working across the whole AI product stack: agent workflows with CrewAI and LangChain, FastAPI services on Google Cloud, model evaluation, and React frontends that make AI features feel approachable. What excites me most is watching an idea go from a whiteboard sketch to something real that people can actually use.

Outside of work I'm a big football fan (the European kind), usually buried in transfer rumors or a Champions League night. When I'm not, I'm gaming, watching movies and anime, reading, or planning my next trip. Same curiosity, different subject, I just like figuring out how things work.
